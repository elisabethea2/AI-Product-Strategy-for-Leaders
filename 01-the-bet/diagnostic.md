# Three-Axis Vulnerability Diagnostic

## Product

**Product:** 
Role Fit — AI-Powered Job Application Copilot
**Your Role:** 
Product Lead

---

## Scores

### Contextual Moat — 2/5
*Workflow depth × switching cost. Would users leave in a weekend if a competitor showed up?*

**Score rationale:** 
Our initial product has a limited contextual moat, as similar AI capabilities are already available through general-purpose tools like ChatGPT.

However, by storing users' CVs, career preferences, and previous job evaluations, we can offer a more personalized experience and introduce some switching costs.

Our longer-term strategy is to strengthen this moat by integrating the entire job application workflow.

**Named attacker (from partner challenge):**
OpenAI (ChatGPT)

---

### Data Advantage — 2/5
*Proprietary signal that compounds with usage. What do you see that OpenAI doesn't?*

**Score rationale:**
Our data advantage comes from building a structured, persistent professional history that goes beyond a traditional CV.

Users can progressively enrich their profiles with detailed project experience, achievements, skills, career preferences, and feedback from previous job evaluations. This context can be reused to generate more relevant fit assessments, tailored CVs, and cover letters.

Unlike general-purpose AI assistants, our product is designed to preserve and reuse this information consistently throughout the job search journey.

However, this advantage is still at an early stage and has not yet been validated.

**Named attacker (from partner challenge):**
LinkedIn (Microsoft)

---

### Platform Exposure — 1/5
*Encroachment risk × pivot speed. If Apple/Google/OpenAI ships your hero feature native — then what?*

**Score rationale:**
Our product faces high platform exposure because LinkedIn already offers AI-powered job compatibility features, such as Top Applicant, and has access to extensive candidate profiles and job listings.

However, these assessments may not always accurately reflect a candidate's actual experience or suitability for a role.

Our differentiation opportunity lies in providing more transparent and explainable fit evaluations, supported by a structured professional history that goes beyond the traditional CV.

Nevertheless, LinkedIn could improve its existing capabilities and introduce similar features, making platform competition a significant strategic risk.

A score of 1/5 indicates very low resilience against platform encroachment and therefore high strategic vulnerability.

**Named attacker (from partner challenge):**
LinkedIn (Microsoft)

---

## Top Vulnerability
Our biggest strategic risk is that established platforms like LinkedIn could replicate our core AI capabilities and leverage their existing user base, making it difficult for us to differentiate and attract users.

## Confidence Level
**Medium**
We have identified a potential differentiation opportunity through structured career history and more transparent job fit evaluations. However, we still need to validate whether users perceive enough additional value compared with existing solutions.
