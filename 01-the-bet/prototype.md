# The Prototype Bet

## What I Built
An AI-powered job application copilot that uses a structured Career Knowledge Base to evaluate job opportunities, identify skill gaps, and provide evidence-based fit assessments.

## Tool Used
- Lovable: Application development, frontend and managed backend.
- Anthropic Claude API: CV extraction, professional content refinement and job-fit analysis.
- Lovable Cloud: Authentication, database, file storage and secure backend functions.

## Prototype Link
https://rolefit.lovable.app/

## AI Value Archetype
Copilot

Role Fit supports users in evaluating opportunities and preparing job applications, while keeping them in control of decisions and professional information.

AI interprets job requirements, retrieves relevant career evidence and generates recommendations. Users review the results, validate their accuracy and decide what to do next.

## The Bet in One Sentence
I believe that a personalized AI job application copilot, powered by a structured professional history, can help job seekers evaluate opportunities more accurately and create tailored applications faster than existing general-purpose tools.

## Kill Criteria
We will evaluate these kill criteria after a 90-day pilot with a representative group of target users. If the criteria are not met after reasonable corrective iterations, we will reconsider the product strategy, scope or continued investment.

- **User Value**: Fewer than 80% of pilot users consider the AI-generated job-fit assessments useful for making application decisions.
- **Accuracy**: The system consistently fails to achieve the 92% accuracy target defined in the Reliability Contract, after corrective iterations.
- **Efficiency**: Users do not achieve at least a 30% reduction in time spent evaluating opportunities and preparing applications.
- **Differentiation**: Users do not perceive a meaningful improvement over existing tools such as LinkedIn or ChatGPT.
- **Trust**: The system repeatedly invents professional experience or generates unsupported claims, even after implementing evidence validation and user review.
