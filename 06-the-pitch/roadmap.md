1. **Validate the bet:** Run the 90-day pilot to measure usefulness, accuracy, repeat usage, time savings and willingness to pay.

### Strategic Conclusion

Role Fit's immediate priority is not expanding AI functionality, but validating its core value proposition and turning personalized career knowledge into a measurable competitive advantage.

## Board Pitch

**Thesis (1 sentence):**

Role Fit can help job seekers create stronger, evidence-based applications by turning their professional experience into a reusable Career Knowledge Base that becomes more valuable with continued use.

**The case:**

1. **Why now:** Role Fit has a functional prototype, but the opportunity to establish differentiation remains unproven. LinkedIn and general-purpose AI tools threaten to commoditize basic CV generation and job matching. We need to validate whether deeper personalization creates sufficient value before committing to scale. The strategy does not yet establish a specific market change in the last 6–12 months.

2. **What's defensible:** Our proposed moat is the evidence-based Career Knowledge Base. Unlike generic AI tools, Role Fit preserves verified professional experience, user corrections and preferences across workflows. However, our Data Flywheel scores only 8/20, and defensibility remains unvalidated. The 90-day pilot must demonstrate measurable improvements from repeated use.

3. **The economics:** At $14.99/month, projected AI-enabled gross margin is 72.8%, with approximately $4.08 in total monthly COGS per user. The proposed model combines subscriptions with usage-based add-ons. However, tripling inference costs reduces projected gross margin to approximately 27.8%, below our 40% stress-test threshold. Pricing acceptance and real usage costs must be validated before scaling.

**The risks:**

1. **Trust / failure modes:** The critical failure is generating fabricated qualifications or misleading career claims that users submit to employers. Role Fit targets ≥92% accuracy, uses an 11-case Golden Dataset including four adversarial cases, flags unsupported claims and requires user review when confidence falls below 70%. Profile changes require explicit approval. These safeguards are designed but not yet validated at production scale.

2. **Scale / governance:** At 10x usage, inference costs, reliability monitoring, privacy obligations and operational oversight become material risks. Role Fit limits AI autonomy to analysis and drafting, requires human approval for verified profile changes and prohibits autonomous job applications. H1 prioritizes cost monitoring, quality monitoring and audit controls before scaling.

3. **Competitive:** LinkedIn could replicate Role Fit's core capabilities and distribute them to its existing user base. If the pilot fails to demonstrate ≥80% usefulness, ≥92% accuracy and measurable personalization benefits after reasonable corrective iterations, we will reconsider further investment. We will also test willingness to pay before committing to commercialization.

**The ask:**

Approve a hypothetical $10,000 budget for a controlled 90-day pilot, supported by one existing Product Lead and part-time engineering capacity.

The pilot will deliver a completed core workflow, operational reliability and cost monitoring, and evidence on usefulness, accuracy, repeat usage, personalization benefits and willingness to pay.

We will review progress at week 6 and make a go/no-go decision at day 90. Expansion into interview preparation and other non-core capabilities will remain paused until the core proposition is validated.
